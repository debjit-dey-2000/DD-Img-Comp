Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Our privacy approach

DD Img Comp is a client-side image compression tool designed to keep your images private. Image conversion and compression take place locally inside your web browser. We do not operate an image-processing server, and your selected images are not uploaded to us.

2. Images and generated files

Your original images, previews, compressed WEBP files, filenames, and ZIP archives remain on your device for the duration of the browser session. Generated files are downloaded only when you request them. Closing or refreshing the page clears the active image queue.

3. Information stored in your browser

The website uses Local Storage to remember interface preferences such as compression quality, dark or light theme, grid or list layout, and animation settings. This information stays in your browser and can be removed by clearing this site's browser data.

4. External resources

Tailwind CSS, JSZip, FileSaver.js, and web fonts may be loaded from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address, browser type, and requested resource. Your images are never included in these requests.

5. Cookies, analytics, and data sales

DD Img Comp stores anonymous aggregate compression statistics, including the number of images processed, total original and compressed byte sizes, processing duration, and server timestamp. This data helps the developer understand daily, weekly, and monthly tool usage. Filenames, image contents, previews, account identifiers, and device identifiers are not included. We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking, or tracking pixels, and we do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

6. External links

The website includes email and LinkedIn links. Visiting an external service is governed by that service's own terms and privacy policy.

7. Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website's features or dependencies change. Questions, privacy concerns, or bug reports can be sent to deydebjit2000@gmail.com.

Your files stay yours.
